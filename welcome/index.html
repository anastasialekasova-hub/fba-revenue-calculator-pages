FBA Revenue Calculator

FBA Revenue Calculator is installed. Open a product on Amazon — its fees and profit appear under the price.

The card shows up by itself on amazon.com and amazon.co.uk product pages, not on the home page. Add your product cost to see your profit — the card remembers it for each product.

Optional: pin the icon

Click the puzzle icon, then the pin next to FBA Revenue Calculator. The icon stays on the toolbar: click it to see the same numbers in a popup, or choose Calculate manually to check fees for any product by its price, size and weight.

No card on the page?

Right-click the page and choose Show FBA Revenue Calculator. The card appears even if you turned cards off in the popup.

Or press Alt+Shift+F (⌥⇧F on a Mac) to show or hide the card. If nothing happens, another extension may already use these keys — set your own at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
